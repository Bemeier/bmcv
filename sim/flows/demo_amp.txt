#!args --rate=200ms --emit=outputs
# Select AMP, then wind channel 0's encoder up and watch out0 come alive.
# Params are picked by tapping a ctrl button while no shift mode is active;
# ctrl ids 0..5 map to FRQ SHP MOD PHS AMP OFS, so SAV selects AMP.
0ms     slider 0.0
50ms    ctrl SAV 1
80ms    ctrl SAV 0
200ms   enc 3 400
2s      end
