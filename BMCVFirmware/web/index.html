BMCV simulator

The firmware's own engine, compiled to WebAssembly. Panel geometry is generated from the hardware's KiCad output.

 

Drag an encoder's ring to turn it, or use the scroll wheel. Click its centre to push. Hold Shift while turning for press-and-turn. Buttons and the slider respond to click and drag.
